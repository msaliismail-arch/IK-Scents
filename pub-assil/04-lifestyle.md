# 04 — Lifestyle

Ton troisième post (femme de profil, N&B) est le plus fort du feed. C'est le seul
qui montre **l'usage**, pas l'objet. Garde cette veine.

---

## ⚠️ Deux règles non négociables

**1. Ne montre jamais un flacon de marque reconnaissable dans une image générée.**
Le flacon dans la main doit être flou, hors champ, ou générique sans détail.
Si tu veux montrer un vrai flacon en situation : photographie-le pour de vrai.

**2. Ne prétends jamais qu'une personne générée est une cliente.**
Une image IA sert à créer une **ambiance**. Un témoignage client se prouve avec un
vrai message, une vraie photo, un vrai prénom. Confondre les deux, c'est exactement
le type de tricherie que ton positionnement « original » interdit.

Si un visuel IA pourrait passer pour une preuve, ne le publie pas.

---

## 4.1 — Profil noir et blanc

**À quoi ça sert :** le visuel de désir. Un tous les 8–10 posts, pas plus : sa
force vient de sa rareté.
**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```
Black and white editorial portrait, close profile view of a woman with eyes closed,
head slightly tilted back, an expression of quiet concentration.
Cropped from mid-neck to just above the forehead, subject positioned on the right third,
negative space on the left. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field.
Dramatic soft side light from the left, deep gradual falloff into shadow on the right side
of the face, no harsh highlights.
Background: seamless dark grey, gradient, no detail.
Clothing: plain dark high-neck top, no pattern, no jewellery.
Palette: monochrome, deep blacks, soft mid greys, luminous skin highlights.
Mood: sensual, restrained, timeless, high fashion editorial.
Ultra photorealistic, medium format look, fine film grain, rich tonal range, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no colour, text, logo, watermark, jewellery, makeup detail, perfume bottle, brand,
harsh contrast, digital smoothness, plastic skin
```

**Variantes :**

- **Homme** — `a man with a short beard, eyes closed, head slightly turned away`
- **Nuque** — `back of a woman's neck and shoulder, hair swept aside` (très élégant, aucun visage à gérer)
- **Poignet** — `a wrist held near the face, fingers relaxed` (sans flacon visible)

## 4.2 — Geste, en couleur

**À quoi ça sert :** montrer l'application sans montrer de marque. Le flacon reste
hors cadre ou flou.
**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```
Editorial lifestyle photograph, close crop on a woman's wrist and forearm held near her
collarbone, fingers relaxed and slightly curled. The face is out of frame.
Shot at eye level, subject on the left third, warm empty space on the right.
85mm lens, very shallow depth of field, sharpest point on the wrist.
Soft natural window light from the left, warm afternoon temperature, gentle shadow on the
skin, no harsh highlights.
Background: warm beige plaster wall, softly out of focus.
Clothing: plain cream linen sleeve, natural folds.
Palette: cream #F7F4EE, sand #EFE8DC, warm skin tones, soft taupe shadow.
Mood: intimate, quiet, sensory, unposed.
Ultra photorealistic, medium format look, fine natural grain, muted film colour grade, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no text, logo, watermark, perfume bottle, brand, jewellery, nail polish, face, blue tones,
oversaturation, plastic skin
```

## 4.3 — Coin d'atelier

**À quoi ça sert :** raconter la marque sans dire un mot. Excellent en image de
couverture de carrousel, ou en visuel « le concept ».
**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```
Editorial lifestyle photograph of a calm perfume atelier corner, vertical.
A cream linen-covered table holding a small ceramic vase with dried botanicals, a folded
cloth, and a shallow brass tray. Shot at a three-quarter angle from standing height.
50mm lens, moderate depth of field, foreground sharp, back wall softly falling off.
Natural daylight from a window on the left casting a soft rectangle of light and a gentle
shadow across the warm beige plaster wall behind.
Palette: cream #F7F4EE, sand #EFE8DC, champagne #D8CBB8, warm taupe, muted sage.
Mood: serene, artisanal, lived-in, expensive without display.
Ultra photorealistic, medium format look, fine grain, muted film colour grade, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no text, logo, watermark, people, perfume bottle, brand, clutter, blue tones, modern
furniture, plastic
```

## 4.4 — Colis, chez le client

**À quoi ça sert :** rassurer avant l'achat. Le paiement à la livraison marche
mieux quand on voit à quoi ressemble ce qui arrive.

**Fais-le en photo réelle dès que possible** — un vrai colis ASSIL sur une vraie
table vaut dix images générées. Ce prompt sert à tenir en attendant.

**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```
Editorial still life photograph viewed from a slight overhead angle.
A small sand-coloured kraft parcel box tied with a thin taupe ribbon, resting on a cream
linen surface. A folded blank card leans against it. A dried flower sprig lies beside.
Composition off-center, box in the lower left, generous empty space upper right.
50mm lens, moderate depth of field.
Soft natural window light from the upper left, warm temperature, gentle contact shadow.
Background: warm beige plaster, softly out of focus.
Palette: cream #F7F4EE, sand #EFE8DC, warm taupe, champagne.
Mood: careful, personal, anticipated.
Ultra photorealistic, sharp texture on the kraft paper, fine grain, muted film grade, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no text, letters, logo, watermark, people, hands, brand, plastic, tape, blue tones, clutter
```
