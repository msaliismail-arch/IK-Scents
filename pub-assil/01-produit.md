# 01 — Produit

**Rappel :** ces prompts génèrent une **scène vide**. Tu détoures ta vraie photo
de flacon et tu la poses dedans (Canva, Photoshop). Le flacon reste réel, le
décor est généré. C'est le seul workflow légal pour un parfum de marque.

Alternative plus rapide : ouvre ta photo dans **Photoshop → Generative Fill**,
sélectionne tout sauf le flacon, et colle le prompt. Le produit n'est pas touché.

---

## 1.1 — Scène crème, lumière de fenêtre

**À quoi ça sert :** post produit standard. Ton visuel par défaut, celui que tu
peux répéter sans lasser.
**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```
A minimalist luxury product photography scene with empty center stage for a perfume bottle.
Soft cream linen textile draped over a low pedestal, centered composition, shot at eye level
with a very slight low angle. 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, background gently blurred.
Soft natural window light entering from the left at 40 degrees, warm afternoon temperature,
long soft shadow falling to the right, no harsh highlights.
Background: warm beige plaster wall, subtle texture, smooth vignette toward the edges.
Props: a single dried white flower and two small dried leaves resting on the linen, far right,
low in frame.
Palette: cream #F7F4EE, sand #EFE8DC, champagne #D8CBB8, warm taupe.
Mood: quiet, editorial, expensive, unhurried.
Ultra photorealistic, medium format look, fine natural grain, muted film colour grade, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no text, logo, watermark, people, hands, bottle, product, clutter, plastic, blue tones,
harsh flash, oversaturation
```

**Variations à tester :**

- **Marbre** — remplace `cream linen textile` par `honed white marble slab with faint grey veining`
- **Soir** — remplace la lumière par `low warm lamp light from the right, deeper shadows, candlelit warmth`
- **Sable** — remplace le fond par `raw beige clay wall with visible hand-applied texture`

```
Palette: sand #EFE8DC, cream #F7F4EE, deep warm shadow, champagne highlights.

Ultra photorealistic, sharp shadow edges, natural contrast, fine grain, 8k.
```

**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```

Polished dark brown stone pedestal, centered, slight low angle, 85mm lens, shallow depth

Background: near-black warm charcoal gradient, no visible edges.
Props: a few scattered dried rose petals at the edge of the light pool, barely lit.
Palette: near-black #171717, warm amber, champagne gold, deep brown.
Mood: opulent, nocturnal, quiet, expensive.
Ultra photorealistic, cinematic colour grade, high contrast, soft bloom on highlights, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no text, logo, watermark, people, bottle, product, blue tones, cold light, busy background
```

## 1.4 — Coffret / packaging (ta propre marque)

**À quoi ça sert :** montrer l'emballage ASSIL. Ici la génération complète est
permise : c'est ton packaging, pas celui d'une marque déposée. Tu poseras ton logo
sur l'emblème vide dans Canva — l'IA écrit mal le texte.
**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 1:1

```
Photorealistic packaging mockup of a rigid square gift box, three-quarter view, standing on
a smooth cream plaster surface.
Matte sand-coloured textured cardstock with a small blank debossed circular emblem area on
the front face and a single thin champagne foil border line near the edge. A slim ribbon in
warm taupe tied around it.
Studio lighting: large softbox from the upper left, subtle rim light along the right edge,
soft contact shadow beneath.
Background: seamless warm cream, gently gradient toward the top.
Palette: sand #EFE8DC, cream #F7F4EE, champagne foil, warm taupe.
Mood: refined, tactile, understated luxury.
Ultra photorealistic, sharp cardstock texture detail, commercial product photography, 8k.
--ar 1:1 --style raw
--no text, letters, brand names, existing logos, wrinkles, cheap gloss, plastic, blue tones
```

## 1.5 — Décants alignés

**À quoi ça sert :** expliquer visuellement ton offre de décants. Ce visuel répond
à « c'est quoi un décant ? » sans une seule ligne de texte.
**Outil :** Midjourney · **Ratio :** 4:5

```
Editorial still life, three small empty clear glass vials of increasing size standing in a
row on a cream linen surface, shot straight on at eye level, evenly spaced.
Each vial has a slim matte black cap. The glass is clean and empty.
Beside them, a glass pipette lying flat and a small folded card, blank.
Soft natural window light from the left, gentle shadows extending right, warm temperature.
Background: warm beige plaster wall, softly out of focus.
Palette: cream #F7F4EE, sand #EFE8DC, clear glass, matte black, champagne.
Mood: precise, clinical but warm, artisanal apothecary.
Ultra photorealistic, macro sharpness on the glass, fine grain, muted film grade, 8k.
--ar 4:5 --style raw
--no text, labels, logos, watermark, people, hands, liquid, colour cast, blue tones, clutter
```
